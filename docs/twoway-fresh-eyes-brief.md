# Fresh-eyes brief — what to look for in a finished two-way episode

You are looking at still frames taken from a FINISHED video, the way a viewer would see it.
You have not been told how it was built, on purpose: you are here to see what is on the
screen, not what was meant to be there. Look at every frame, at full size.

## What the episode is
A Practical Punting YouTube episode (horse-racing strategy, Australian). Two presenters read
an old magazine conversation aloud: **Gordon** (reading Brian Blackwell) and **Steve**
(reading Barry Meadow). The screen moves between the two of them side by side ("the
two-box"), one of them full frame, motion-graphic cards with text, racing b-roll footage,
a title card at the start, an end card and a warranty slide at the end.

## The faults people have actually found (look hardest for these)
1. **Something under the corner logo.** The big Practical Punting logo sits in one corner
   for the whole film. Any card text, picture or caption running underneath or touching
   it is a fault. (EP56: a card's line "At others" ran under it.)
2. **The corner logo missing** on any frame before the end card is fully in — including
   the title card, cards and b-roll.
3. **Numbers on saddlecloths or riders.** Horses in the b-roll must carry plain cloths: any
   numeral or lettering on a saddlecloth, rider or breeches is a fault, however small.
4. **Overlaps and collisions.** Two graphics on top of each other; a name chip over a card;
   text running off a card or off the screen; a cut-off word.
5. **A missing nameplate.** When Gordon first speaks as himself his name should be on
   screen; when the two-box first appears each man's "reading …" chip should be there.
6. **Anything a viewer would find odd:** a frozen or black frame, a face or hand that is
   wrong, more than four horses or people crowding a shot, a horse with the wrong number of
   legs, a racing rail between the camera and the field, text you cannot read at this size.

## By design — do NOT report these
(Learned from the first test run, 13 Nov 2026: each was raised as a MAYBE on a correct film.)
- The corner logo sitting over a **presenter or a background photo** — only TEXT or GRAPHICS
  under it are faults.
- The **end card / e-book slide** at the very end carrying its own smaller logo instead of the
  big corner one.
- A presenter in the same pose in frames minutes apart — the listening footage loops.
- The "reading …" chips showing only on a presenter's first appearances, and their small top
  bars differing in colour (it marks who is speaking).
- The racecourse rails seen through the studio window behind Gordon (a backdrop, not b-roll).

## How to report
- One line per finding: **the frame's file name (it carries the time), what you see, and
  where on the frame.** Plain words.
- Say how sure you are: SURE or MAYBE.
- If you find nothing in a category, say so in one line — do not invent findings to fill it.
- You do not approve or reject anything. You report what you see.
