## What was NOT voiced, and why (§0a-i selection rule)

Nothing was repaired. Two passages were left out of the spoken track; both are the
article's and both must appear verbatim in `ebook/body.html`.

**1. The two computer sentences.** The transcription reads:

> *"I have two corn punters; one is linked to the Telecom Viatel service … The other
> compute has my special programme for the tri fecta betting."*

`corn punters` and `compute` are scan damage for *computers*. Repairing them would be a
category-2 call and there is **no repair-disclosure header on this capture**, so the
clause was dropped instead, which needs no departure and alters nothing. Speaking
*"corn punters"* aloud was the other option and it is nonsense to the ear. The Warren
Block sentence that follows was kept — it is clean and it is the useful part (he buys
his ratings in).

**2. "I do all the work myself."** The article qualifies it immediately:

> *"I do all the work myself. have a chap who helps me with the corn punter programmes
> themselves."*

The qualifier carries the same scan damage. **Dropping only the qualifier would have
left Gordon saying Scott works entirely alone, which the article does not say** — so
both sentences went, per the second condition of the selection rule (*the remaining
sentence must still read honestly*).
